Logistics

Finalists who are interested to apply for the masterclasses will receive their lesson(s) either:

  • • Live in Brussels directly after the competition on 21st of December. Prof. Raskin will exceptionally teach this year on the 21st, 22nd and 23rd of December due to the very high number of applications.
  • • Digitally for participants who are unable to travel.
  • • The duration of one lesson is of 50 minutes.

For pianists not participating in the finals of the competition, some lessons will be planned for December 18.
